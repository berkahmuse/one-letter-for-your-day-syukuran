Entry for the [One Letter for Your Day](https://make.fun/c/u-puan-one-letter-for-your-day-e54e9c36)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun): send exactly one letter, A to Z,
standing for the word that describes your day.

## The entry

`main.py` writes exactly one character — the letter **S** — to
`/output/entry.txt`, and nothing else: no word, no gloss, no punctuation,
no trailing newline.

## The word behind the letter

**S is for "snooze"**: the day the alarm went off and the hand kept
slapping snooze, again and again, until half the day was already gone.
Vivid (the alarm, the dark room, the reaching hand), funny (you snoozed
your own day away — the kind of defeat you repeat to a friend), and true
to a real, ordinary morning. S fits the sound of the word itself.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt   # S
```

Requires Python 3. No dependencies, no network, no secrets.
